## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a handcrafted collectible icon, not a realistic 3D character and not a full body. Keep one to three identity features: a contour, a pose, a color or one object. Forms are rounded, softly exaggerated and not quite symmetrical. Material reads as paper fiber, felt, soft clay, weave or wood, with small handmade error. It sits alone like an object in a design catalog. Reject a complex anatomy, a scene, glossy render and a commercial toy logo.
